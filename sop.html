Step 1: Introduction & Inspiration
Tip: Start with an impactful story or observation that inspired you to pursue this field.
Step 2: Academic Background & Achievements
Tip: Detail your degree, papers, or internships. Show both the hard skills and soft skills you gained from these experiences.
Step 3: Why This Program & University?
Tip: How does the program align with your goals? Mention specific university features like labs or industry connections.
Step 4: Career Goals
Tip: Present your future plans in a realistic way. What is your short-term goal right after graduation, and what is your long-term vision?
Step 5: Conclusion & Expectations
Tip: Keep it short and impactful. Express your hope, confidence, and readiness to contribute to their institution.
