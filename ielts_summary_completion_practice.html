9 Essential Strategies

01 Fill in the Gaps

You will see a paragraph with missing words. Your job is to listen for them.

"How ___ affects climate change" -> Answer: Human Activity.
02 Summary, Not Exact

The text summarizes ideas. Words on the page may differ from the audio, but meaning is the same.

"New law to reduce..." = "Regulations to cut down air pollution". Answer: Pollution.
03 Use the Title

The title focuses your mind on the main topic.

"The cause of obesity" -> Topic relates to healthcare/lifestyle.
04 Follow Order

Answers appear in the same order as the recording.

05 Word Limit

Strictly check word limits (e.g., "NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS").

06 Grammar Check

Predict word type (Noun/Verb). Ensure grammatical fit.

"The delay was..." -> Noun (Bad Weather). "The company..." -> Verb (Increased).
07 Paraphrasing

Listen for synonyms. Words change but meaning stays.

"Experience stress" = "Cause pressure". Stress = Pressure.
08 Exact Words

Listen exactly to the words you hear for the answer itself.

09 Checklist

1. Read title. 2. Predict word type. 3. Listen for keywords. 4. Check grammar. 5. Check spelling.
